A fraction represents a part of a whole or any number of equal parts. It consists of a numerator (top number) and a denominator (bottom number). 
To add or subtract fractions, they must have a common denominator. If denominators are different, find the Least Common Multiple (LCM) to convert them. 
When multiplying fractions, multiply the numerators together and denominators together. When dividing, multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction.
